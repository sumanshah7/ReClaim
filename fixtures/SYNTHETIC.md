# Synthetic fixtures

Every file in this folder is invented for the demo. Names, orders, amounts and ticket images are fake. Column layouts are plausible stand-ins, not copies of real platform exports. They are not data from Playa Bowls, Chutney or any real restaurant.

## Restaurant samples

Two samples are named after restaurants the project has been discussed with: **Chutney (Montclair, NJ)**, a momo-focused DoorDash sample (`chutney_montclair_doordash_payout.csv`), and **Tandoori Flames (Parsippany, NJ)**, a biryani and tandoori Uber Eats sample (`tandoori_flames_parsippany_ubereats_payout.csv`). The orders, customers, amounts, prices and ticket images are invented, and the menu items are plausible examples, not those restaurants' real menus. This is not data from either restaurant and says nothing about how either of them operates.
