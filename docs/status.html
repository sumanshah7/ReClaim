Reclaim

Status report: what was built, what is complete, what remains. All data is synthetic.

1. What it is

Independent restaurants lose money when DoorDash, Uber Eats or Grubhub deduct a refund after a "missing or wrong item" claim, and they have no back office to contest it. Reclaim reads the payout CSV, scores each refund deduction by recoverable amount × win probability (deadline breaks ties), gathers evidence from tickets and timestamps, drafts a factual dispute, and waits for the owner's approval. Nothing is sent without approval. Targets: Peri Forward Deployed Challenge, LangChain Best Use of deepagents, optionally Photon.

4. Honest limits

  • Replay covers only the recorded decision paths (approve first then skip, or approve all). A new CSV needs a live model and API key.
  • Win probabilities are heuristics, not measured platform win rates. The UI says "estimates, not guarantees".
  • Deadlines are counted from the payout date; platform rules should be confirmed before relying on them.
  • The text approval and phone are simulated; do not describe them as live.
